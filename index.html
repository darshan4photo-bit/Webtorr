    How it works

    🧲

    1. Add a torrent

    Paste a magnet link or drop a .torrent file. The browser fetches metadata from peers.

    ⚡

    2. Stream as it downloads

    Pieces are requested in playback order through a Service Worker, so video starts within seconds.

    🔒

    3. Stays in your tab

    Everything runs client-side over WebRTC. Nothing is uploaded to our server and nothing is stored on disk.

    FAQ

    Why is my torrent stuck on “Connecting…”?

    Browsers can only talk to peers that support WebRTC (WebTorrent peers). Classic-only torrents (plain BitTorrent clients over TCP/UDP) usually have no browser-reachable peers. Torrents that are also seeded by WebTorrent clients — like the samples above — work great. For best results, use a torrent with a wss:// tracker.

    Which formats can I play?

    With the Server engine, essentially all of them: MP4, WebM, MKV, AVI, WMV, FLV, MOV, MPEG/TS — and audio like MP3, FLAC, OGG, WAV, AAC and Opus. If your browser can't decode a file natively (HEVC/H.265, DivX, AC3/DTS audio…), it is converted to H.264/AAC on the fly. Audio-only files with embedded cover art are handled too. The Browser P2P engine is limited to whatever your browser plays natively: MP4 (H.264/AAC), WebM, MP3, OGG, FLAC, WAV, images, and text subtitles (.srt / .vtt are auto-detected). MKV and HEVC depend on your browser.

    Is it legal?

    This tool is just a client, like any torrent app. Only stream content you have the right to access — public-domain, Creative Commons, or your own files.

    Do you store my files?

    No. Data lives in your browser's memory while the tab is open, and is discarded when you close it.
